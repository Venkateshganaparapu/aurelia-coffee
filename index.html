Single-Origin Highland Cultivation.

02

Selective Hand-Harvesting.

Skilled estate pickers hand-select only perfectly ripe ruby cherries over multiple passes through the trees, ensuring zero astringency in the cup.

03

Intentional Drum Roasting.

Roasted in micro-batches in our Bengaluru roastery using customized roast profiles that highlight origin acidity, delicate florals, and natural caramel sweetness.
